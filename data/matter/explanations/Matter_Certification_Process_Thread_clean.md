#### **Matter Certification Process Detail**

**M1: Request a unique Manufacturer ID Code (Vendor ID) from Alliance Certification.** Requests should be emailed to certification@csa-iot.org. The database of assigned Manufacturer ID codes is located on Causeway: https://groups.csa-iot.org/wg/members-all/document/10905

Updates to the database are published at the beginning of each month. Assigned codes may be used immediately, but will not appear in the published database until the next scheduled update.

Estimated time to completion: 1-2 business days.

**M2: Register a Distributed Compliance Ledger (DCL) account**

**M3: Enter the corresponding Model-Version entry for the product that will be the subject of the certification application**. Further resources for geƫng started with the DCL, including a how-to guide, are available in the Matter Resource Kit: https://community.csa-iot.org/page/matter-resource-kit Note: The Model-Version may be entered at any time prior to final registration on the DCL. You may enter this immediately or wait until after approval of the certification application.

Estimated time to completion: 2-3 business days (includes approval of Vendor account)

**M4: Obtain/complete all dependent certifications for implemented Matter network transports.** This includes Thread certification for Matter-over-Thread devices as described above and also includes Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (for commissioning), if applicable.

**M5: Submit your product to an authorized test service provider for formal certification testing**. A list of authorized providers may be found here: https://csa-iot.org/certification/testing-providers/ The provider will send the final test report directly to us when testing is complete. If there are any issues with testing or failed tests, you will need to work with your test provider to resolve them until your device has successfully passed all testing.

Estimated time to completion: 2-3 weeks from the start of the testing period. Note that the start of testing is determined by your test provider and the availability of open test slots. Contact individual test providers for more information.

**M6: Submit a certification application online via the Alliance Certification Tool**. Instructions for accessing the tool and submitting an application can be found in the user information guide available on Causeway:

https://groups.csa-iot.org/wg/members-all/document/129

Note that all applications require submission of completed Declaration of Conformity (DoC) and PICS documents. Document links: https://community.csa-iot.org/page/certification

Your application will be reviewed, and upon approval, you will receive a formal certificate of compliance and a listing on the Alliance database of certified products.

Estimated time to completion: 2 weeks from (1) completion of payment and (2) receipt of the final test report from your test provider. Average time until first action is approximately four business days. "First action" is either approval (if there are no issues with the application after review) or an e-mail communication back to you with a list of items that need to be addressed to continue processing. In the latter case, time to approval is dependent upon the specific issue(s) identified and your response time in addressing them.

**M7: Receive Certification Declaration (CD) file**. When the CD blob file for the product is ready, a separate email notification will be sent with instructions for download (the file will be available in the Certification Tool from the account that submitted the application). No action by you is required to initiate this process.

Estimated time to completion: 3 business days

**M8: Request DCL compliance entry by Alliance**. After you have entered the DCL Model-Version entry for your certified product (see above), log into the Certification Tool, edit the product in the DCL Management page, set the DCL Record Entered field to "Yes," and submit. The Alliance will then enter the certified product record in the DCL. Note that you must first receive your CD blob file before starting this process. All Matter certified products are made publicly available through the DCL via https://webui.dcl.csa-iot.org

Estimated time to completion: 1 business day

#### **Thread Certification Process Detail**

**T1: Submit a Thread product inheritance certification application.** The inheritance application can be found on the Thread Group portal:

https://portal.threadgroup.org/MyWorkGroups/Documents.aspx?Type=Detail&DocumentID=2790

If pursuing a Type III inheritance, make sure the application includes a valid certified component CID and the appropriate Thread device role support detail; check with your chipset supplier if you need help.

Estimated time to completion: 1 week (if all requirements are fulfilled)

**T2: Data review and certification grant.** Provide all required supporting information and any clarifying detail. Your information will be reviewed, and upon approval, you will receive a formal certificate of compliance, a Thread certification logo license and a listing on the Thread Group website.

Estimated time to completion: 1-2 weeks
