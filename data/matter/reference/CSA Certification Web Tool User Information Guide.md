#### <span id="page-5-0"></span>Deciding How to Certify

#### There are currently four certification methods available to Alliance Members

- Certify A New Product
  - Used by Members to certify single Fully Tested New Products with the CSA
- Certification by Similarity
  - Used to certify products that are Similar to a Product that the Member has already certified. The base function of the device must remain the same, but may include minor additional features or changes that do not effect Zigbee functionality. For more information, see the CSA Certification Policy, available at <a href="https://csa-iot.org/resources/governing-documents/">https://csa-iot.org/resources/governing-documents/</a>
- Family Certification
  - Used to certify a product family (parent product plus additional product variants). Variants of the Same Product may be certified in a single application. For more information about the program and allowable variations between family members, see the CSA Certification Policy
- Certification Transfer Program
  - Used when rebranding/white labeling another previously certified product from another Member to maintain the new product's Certified status

For additional information on different certification types available please visit

https://csa-iot.org/certification/why-certify/

https://csa-iot.org/become-member/

# <span id="page-40-0"></span>Certification Transfer Program

- The Certification Transfer Program (CTP) allows for "white labeling" or rebranding an existing certified product from a Participant or Promoter Member with that Member's authorization
- The original certified product owner must first update the specific product used as the basis for CTP to allow the product into the Certification Transfer Program. For detailed instructions, [see the Certification Web Tool User Guide section entitled Certification Transfer Program –](#page-20-0) Enrolling a Product
- Note: Associate level members do not have access to the Certification Web Tool and must use the Associate Certification Tool to submit CTP applications: <https://zigbeecertifiedproducts.knack.com/zigbee-certified#associate-certification-tool/>
