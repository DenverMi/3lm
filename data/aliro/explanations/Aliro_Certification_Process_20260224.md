## Aliro Certification Process

### Membership

Aliro certification is open to Alliance Member companies. Applicant companies will be granted Aliro certified status for products that pass certification. Certification benefits include a license to use the Aliro certification mark, a RANDz intellectual property license to technology in implemented specifications, and public listing of the certified product on the Alliance website.

For new products certified by testing, membership at Adopter level or higher is required. To join the Alliance, send your membership inquiry to Alliance Member Services.

### ATL Testing

The first step is to develop your products and submit them for testing at an authorized test provider. 

Please contact providers individually for programs offered, quotes, testing schedule, and submission requirements.

Note: Successful SVE participation meets the certification requirement for new product certification.

### Dependent Certification

For Aliro, you will also need to obtain certifications for your implemented network transports (dependent certifications). You can obtain these from the associated standards organizations. For more information, review the Alliance Certification Policy.

For each supported transport, you will need to complete a network transport attestation form for submission to the Alliance with your Aliro application. Network transport forms are located here

### Submission and Review

Once the application is completed, please submit for review. The Alliance will process your application and, upon approval, issue the certification. You will receive a certificate of compliance and your product will immediately be listed on the public database of certified products (unless requested otherwise in the application form).
